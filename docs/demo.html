The library, drawing what it computes

Every number on this board came out of the compiled library: the positions from its layout, the distances from its searches, the highlighted edges from its tree. The page places what it is told and nothing more.

What you are looking at

Pick a graph, then an algorithm. For the searches, click a node to start from it. The number under a node is the answer for that node — hops, cost, colour, piece or share.
